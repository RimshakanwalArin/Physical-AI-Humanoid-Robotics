# Introduction to Physical AI

## Embodied Intelligence

Embodied intelligence refers to the idea that intelligence emerges from the interaction of an agent with its environment through physical embodiment. Rather than treating cognition as abstract symbol manipulation, embodied approaches recognize that an intelligent system's structure, morphology, and interaction with the environment are central to its cognitive abilities.

### Key Concepts

- **Perception**: How robots sense their environment through sensors
- **Action**: How robots affect their environment through actuators
- **Interaction**: Dynamic feedback loop between sensing and acting

## Principles of Physical AI

Physical AI systems are designed around several core principles:

1. **Real-World Constraints**: Systems must account for physical laws, friction, dynamics
2. **Embodied Design**: The robot's body affects what it can learn and do
3. **Continuous Adaptation**: Real-world environments are constantly changing
4. **Multi-Modal Integration**: Combining vision, touch, proprioception, and other modalities

## Real-World AI

Physical agents must perceive and act in real-world environments, which introduces constraints and opportunities absent from purely symbolic systems. This course explores how to build intelligent systems that can navigate, manipulate, and interact with the physical world.
